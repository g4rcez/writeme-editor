The idea

Write Me is a digital workshop for thought — not a word processor, not a productivity suite. It's a clean space where the only thing that matters is the quality of your thinking. No toolbars fighting for your attention. No cloud syncing your private ideas. Just clarity, speed, and the words you mean to write.

What's inside

04 features
01

True Privacy

No servers, no trackers, no accounts. Everything lives on your device. Your thoughts are yours alone.

02

Zero Distraction

A minimalist interface designed to disappear. No toolbars, no popups — a blank canvas that gets out of your way.

03

Pure Markdown

Your notes are plain text. Portable, future-proof, readable by any tool — now and in twenty years.

04

AI, Privately

Bring your own model. Math, diagrams, code, chat — all the power, none of the surveillance.
