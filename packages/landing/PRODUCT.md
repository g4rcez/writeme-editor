# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People who write to think and want a place to capture and return to notes, ideas, drafts, tasks, and saved reading.

## Product Purpose

Write Me is a note-taking and writing editor for collecting and developing notes, ideas, drafts, tasks, and saved links. Success means people can stay with an idea, then find their work again.

## Positioning

Write Me combines a Markdown writing workspace with keyboard-driven navigation, AI assistance, read-later capture, and local-first note storage.

## Operating Context

People create and organize notes, save links to revisit, and work in the web/PWA or desktop app. The app supports filesystem and database-backed storage modes.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The editor supports Markdown, keyboard-driven navigation, search, AI assistance, daily notes, tasks, snippets, drafts, and saved links.
- People can customize themes, templates, panels, folders, shortcuts, and editor size.
- Notes are local-first and available in formats people control. Storage can use the local filesystem or a database.
- AI integrations can connect to configured providers. Do not imply that every AI request stays on-device.
- The current desktop release is v1.1.0: Windows x64, Apple Silicon macOS (unsigned), and Linux .deb/.rpm packages.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is Write Me.
- Write Me's primary brand color is purple; green must not replace it as the primary color.
