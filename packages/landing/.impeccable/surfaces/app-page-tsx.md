# Landing page

## Scope and visitor mode

`app/page.tsx` · Persuade.

## Audience, job, and action

People who write to think. Help them recognize Write Me as a place for notes, drafts, and ideas, then start in the web app or choose a desktop download.

## Proof and constraints

Show the real app screenshot (`public/writeme-home-hero.png`). Keep the hero's hover-reactive hive field and Write Me's purple as the primary brand color; do not use green as the main accent. Use supported product facts only; do not invent reviews, usage figures, or performance claims. Describe local-first storage accurately: configured AI providers may receive content sent to them.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A writing workspace should hold one clear strip of attention: show the real product doing its job, with useful tools close and the rest of the page quiet.

**OWN-WORLD:** Near-black ink, warm silver type, and Write Me purple used as the light leak and action color. The hero's hexagonal field stays visible and responds to fine-pointer hover. Use framed imagery and fine separators as a restrained nod to a hand-processed contact strip; never let that frame obscure the real product screen. Keep surfaces open rather than building the page from feature tiles.

**STORY:** Visitors see Write Me's real workspace, understand that it holds notes and drafts with search, shortcuts, and AI assistance nearby, then choose the web app or a platform download. Follow with a scannable feature index, an honest local-first explanation, and desktop downloads.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A compact sticky navigation sits above a viewport-height hero. Set the headline and short explanation in a left column with a purple primary action and a quiet desktop-download link. Give the actual dashboard screenshot the larger right column. Let the hive field span behind both columns without dimming its hover response. At phone widths, stack the headline, actions, then screenshot; keep the grid visible as a still background.

**FORM:** Candidate 6 from seed `7c3bf8c5`, the hand-processed 16mm film contact sheet. Translate the silver emulsion and near-black leader to the user's ink palette, and translate the light leak to Write Me purple. The requested hive remains the signature interaction; frame-like rules carry the contact-sheet grammar through the remaining sections. Keep copy, hierarchy, and primary actions literal enough to identify a writing editor immediately.
